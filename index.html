Family Verification Protocol

Unexpected request? Stop. End the incoming call or message. Do not move money, share codes, install software, or follow contact details provided in the request.

  1. Call back using a number or channel verified before the incident.
  2. Complete the family's offline verification step.
  3. Require the pre-agreed second approver for high-impact requests.
  4. If verification fails, alert the household and the relevant institution.
